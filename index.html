Simple Tips for Managing your Budget

Make a Budget

Write down your income and your expenses each month. A simple guide is 50% for needs , 30% for wants and 20% for savings, which you can adjust to fit your life.

Build an emergency Fund

Set aside some money for unexp;ected costs such as medical bills and sudden job loss.Many people aim to save 3 to 6 months of livibng expenses over time.

Save before you Spend

When your income arrives, move a small amount intol savings first.
